# Editorial architecture: Beyond Words (2026)

## What is historically verified
The 24 May 2022 Meta Model manuscript explicitly labels Part 1 (communication model / map is not the territory), Part 2 (language, interpretation and meta model) and Part 3 (dynamic Meta Model after Michael Breen). Part 2 appears after Part 3 in the file's physical order. The original title of Part 4 has **not been verified**. The fourth part below is a reconstruction for the revised edition, not a recovered historical title.

## Four-part working plan
### Part I: The map and language
How people represent and interpret experience; map/territory, linguistic model, deletion, generalization, distortion; historical roots and evidence limits.
### Part II: Classical Meta Model in dialogue
Patterns and clarifying questions; differences between eliciting information, challenging an inference and acknowledging emotion; full worked examples and practice.
### Part III: Dynamic Meta Model (Michael Breen)
Breen's spatial table and five rows; NLP Compass (abstraction vs detail; inside vs outside the map); Framing Tool; intentional navigation during live conversations; do not reduce this to one survey chapter.
### Part IV: Prisms and Beyond Words
Haggai Sofer's prism approach; choosing a cognitive lens even without a literal pattern violation; 2026 Answers Model (prism -> anchor question -> mapping answer -> redirect/pivot); state work, Overdurf, Sleight of Mouth, agency, rapport risks and critical integration.

## AI companion, not a separate historical part
Each part has practice exercises that can be simulated in ChatGPT using FICTIONAL, non-identifying content. AI proposes alternatives and roleplays; it does not diagnose, conduct unsupervised psychotherapy or automatically infer hidden causes. A standalone chapter critically covers evidence, limits and privacy.

## Chapter template
Core claim; original attribution; example dialogue; common mistake; decision point; app exercise ID; possible AI practice; scientific confidence label; source path; editorial status.

## Key distinctions
Breen COMPASS = navigation direction; BREEN TABLE = spatial organization of patterns; FRAMING TOOL = broader context/goals/maintaining factors; SOFER PRISMS = intentional lens chosen by practitioner; ANSWERS MODEL 2026 = a proposed four-step practical protocol. These names are related but not interchangeable.
