## Verified teaching sequence

- **B 00:50–02:05**: Teach the movement sequence before inserting a personal life situation. CRASH contraction -> COACH centering/grounding. Feet to earth, head upward, shoulders relax but do not collapse.
- **B 02:30–03:07**: Reactivity -> openness. Demonstrate reaction; then hands move in giving and receiving. The teacher says participants may choose another movement.
- **B 03:12–03:54**: Analysis paralysis -> awareness, observing and listening.
- **B 04:00–04:43**: Separation/disconnection -> togetherness/connection.
- **B 04:43–05:37**: Hostility or pain -> holding/hospitality toward experience, like holding a baby.
- **B 05:37–06:34**: Review the COACH sequence alone.
- **D 00:00–01:35, 03:44–04:48 and 05:50–06:13**: Four hospitality statements are offered as practice: interest in what arises, something arising and perhaps healing, confidence in some underlying logic, and welcoming what comes. **These are not clinical guarantees.**
- **E 00:28–03:43**: A single connected gentle-attention practice: grounding -> openness (giving/receiving) -> awareness of environment and body -> connecting -> holding.
- **E 03:43–05:19**: Incorporates the hospitality statements; concludes with a grounding return.

## Content policies for the app

1. The short axis label stays brief: e.g. **החזקה**. A details view can expand to **החזקה, הכלה ונוכחות**; this does not mean the title is inconsistent.
2. The two exercise modes are not "invent a new guided visualization" vs "body." They are **the movement in ordinary attention** and **the same movement with inward/gentle attention**, modeled on lessons B and E.
3. The avatar manipulation, feelings check-in, personal situations, prompts, and transfer-to-life stage are **our app's educational extensions**, not represented as material explicitly recorded in the course.
4. Never add a new image, affirmation, therapeutic intervention or promise and attribute it to the course without identifying a matching timestamp.
5. A first-time user should be offered a simple path through the five movements, while allowing a specific movement to be chosen.
6. The screen must not suggest that experiencing discomfort or doing a gesture guarantees nervous-system change, unconscious reprogramming, or healing.
